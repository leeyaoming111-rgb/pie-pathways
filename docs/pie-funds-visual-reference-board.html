Recurring patterns to match

  • Dark scene or marble background + white circular badge/logo + single green label — the core campaign composition.
  • Device mockups (laptop, tablet, phone) used to present digital products like the newsletter and video updates.
  • Black-and-white or desaturated photography for editorial/content headers (Auckland skyline, charts).
  • Gold appears only in props and award contexts — it signals performance and the premium Wealth tier.
  • Video is a first-class content type (play-button thumbnails with labelled banners).
  • Green usage rule of thumb: ribbons, small banners, tags — never backgrounds or large fills.
